Amazon Order History Dashboard

Drop in Order History.csv from Amazon to visualize spending trends, categories, top purchases, and order activity.

Privacy note: this page parses your CSV entirely in your browser memory. No account, database, server upload, or tracking is required. Refreshing or clearing the page removes the loaded data.

How to get the CSV from Amazon

  1. Go to amazon.com and sign in.
  2. Open Accounts & Lists → Account → Data & Privacy / Request Your Information.
  3. Request your order or retail purchase history data.
  4. When the export is ready, download the ZIP/CSV.
  5. Extract Order History.csv, then drag it here.

Amazon labels vary by region/account. If you already have an Order History Report CSV, use it directly.
